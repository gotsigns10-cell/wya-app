Common Questions
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your vendor dashboard anytime. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your listing stays active on the free tier.
Is the free plan really free?
Yes, always. You pay a 12% commission only on completed, paid orders. No monthly fee, no credit card needed to start.
What's the Founding Vendor Guarantee?
If you sign up for Pro or Growth now, you won't be charged a monthly fee until WYA delivers your first 5 real paid orders. We don't make money until you make money.
What is the Ambassador program?
Refer other vendors to WYA and earn 20-25% monthly recurring commission on every active referral. Available on the Growth plan.
When do I get paid?
Payouts are processed every week via Stripe, direct to your bank account. Growth vendors get priority processing.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes. Upgrade instantly from your dashboard. Downgrade at the end of your billing cycle. Prorated credits for mid-cycle changes.
Do customers need to download an app?
No. WYA is a web app that works on any phone browser. Customers find you on the map instantly — no download needed.
Is WYA only for food trucks?
No. WYA is for all mobile and pop-up vendors — food trucks, barbers, tattoo artists, craft vendors, mobile detailers, market sellers, event vendors, and more. Anyone who sets up shop temporarily.
