Frequently Asked Questions

How do I go live as a vendor?
Log into your vendor dashboard and tap the "I'm Open" button. Make sure to allow location access so customers can find you on the map.
Why can't customers see me on the map?
Check that: (1) Your GPS toggle is ON, (2) Location permissions are enabled in your browser/phone settings, (3) You have a stable internet connection.
How do I add menu items?
Go to your vendor dashboard, tap the Menu tab, and tap "Add Item". You must have at least one menu item before going live.
How do payments work?
WYA uses Stripe for secure payment processing. Customers can pay by card when ordering ahead. You'll receive payouts to your connected bank account.
Does WYA drain my battery?
GPS usage does consume battery. WYA updates your location every 15 seconds when live. For best battery life, close the app when you're done for the day.
How do I delete my account?
Email us at support@universoul.io with your account email address and we'll delete your data within 24 hours.

About WYA

WYA (Where You At) is a real-time vendor discovery app built for Santa Barbara. We help customers find local food trucks, pop-up shops, and mobile vendors — exactly when and where they're open.

Made with love by Universoul LLC in Santa Barbara, CA.
