Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

WYA ("Where You At") is operated by Universoul LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our vendor discovery app.

Information We Collect

For Customers:

  • Location data (when you allow) to show vendors near you and calculate distances
  • Account information (email, name) if you create an account
  • Order history and favorites

For Vendors:

  • Business information (name, category, description, hours)
  • Location data when your GPS toggle is ON
  • Menu items and pricing
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)

How We Use Location Data

Customers: Your location is only used while the app is open to show nearby vendors and calculate distances. We do not track your location history.

Vendors: When you toggle "I'm Open", your GPS location is broadcast to customers so they can find you. Location updates occur every 15 seconds while you're live. When you toggle off, your location is no longer shared.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With Stripe for secure payment processing
  • With vendors when you place an order (your name and order details)
  • If required by law or to protect our users

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure authentication tokens, and regular security audits. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your account data at any time
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Disable location sharing at any time

Children's Privacy

WYA is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
